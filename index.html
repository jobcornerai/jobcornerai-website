FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is JobCorner AI the employer?

No. JobCorner AI is an independent referral guide. All opportunities are on Mercor, which runs applications, interviews, selection, contracts and payment.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No — applying is free, and JobCorner AI charges experts nothing at any stage. Be cautious of anyone who asks for payment to access job applications.

Am I guaranteed to get work?

No, and any site promising that isn't being straight with you. Selection is by Mercor based on your profile, assessments and live project demand.

Why don't the cards show pay rates?

Rates depend on the exact live role, your expertise and location, and they change. Mercor shows the real terms for each role when you apply — we'd rather send you to the true number than publish one that might not apply to you.

What do I need to apply on Mercor?

Typically a profile describing your expertise, plus Mercor's AI interview and any assessments for the role. A LinkedIn profile and an up-to-date resume help you move faster.

Can I apply from outside Canada?

These categories are remote and Mercor works with experts globally, though eligibility for a specific role is set by Mercor and shown on the role itself.
