About Us

The corner where human expertise meets AI.

JobCorner AI is a talent studio built for the expert economy: we connect skilled professionals with remote AI-training contract work, delivered through our partner AI research platform.

Our mission
“To make human expertise the easiest thing in the world for AI to learn from — and the easiest work in the world for experts to find.”

Every breakthrough model is trained, tested and corrected by people: the linguist who hears the wrong tone, the engineer who spots the subtle bug, the clinician who knows the dangerous answer from the safe one. Our mission is to shorten the distance between those people and the teams who need them.

Who we are

A new studio, built deliberately for this moment.

JobCorner AI is a new talent and referral studio — created because the way AI gets built has changed, and the way experts find that work hasn't kept up. Frontier AI labs now rely on human judgement at enormous scale: native speakers who can evaluate a model in their own language, developers who can grade its code, doctors, lawyers and analysts who can tell a confident answer from a correct one. Our partner AI research platform hires those experts for remote contract projects. The demand is real, the work is meaningful — but for most professionals, the path in is genuinely unclear.

Which category fits me? What does the work actually involve day to day? What happens after I apply — and who decides? Those questions are where talented people stall, and they're exactly the questions JobCorner AI exists to answer. We organise the opportunity landscape in plain language, prepare experts for each step of the platform process, and send them through the right door with their eyes open.

Everything we publish is written to be used: what each category of work involves day to day, who it suits, what the application process asks of you, and what a strong profile looks like. No hype, no inflated promises — just a clear, honest map of a genuinely new kind of work, drawn by a studio that studies this market every day.

We are deliberately independent. We are not an employer of the experts we guide, and we don't pretend to be a giant agency with a decade of invented history. We are a focused, modern studio that would rather be precise about what it does than impressive about what it doesn't. That honesty is not a limitation — it's the product.

About JobCorner AI

Is JobCorner AI the employer?

No. For expert opportunities, JobCorner AI is an independent referral guide. All opportunities are delivered through our partner AI research platform, which runs applications, interviews, selection, contracts and payment.

How does JobCorner AI fit into the process?

We curate the opportunity categories, explain them in plain language, prepare you for each step, and link you directly to our partner AI research platform to apply. See the About page for the full picture.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No — applying is free, and JobCorner AI charges experts nothing at any stage. Be cautious of anyone who asks for payment to access job applications.

Applying

What do I need to apply?

Typically a profile describing your expertise, plus an AI interview and any assessments for the role. A LinkedIn profile and an up-to-date resume help you move faster.

Am I guaranteed to get work?

No, and any site promising that isn't being straight with you. Selection is made by the hiring partner, based on your profile, assessments and live project demand.

Can I apply from outside Canada?

These categories are remote and Our partner platform works with experts globally, though eligibility for a specific role is set by the hiring partner and shown on the role itself.

Can I apply to more than one category?

Your profile on the partner platform covers your full background. Start with the category that matches your strongest expertise — the role matcher on the Opportunities page helps you pick — and make sure your profile reflects everything you can expertly judge.

Pay & terms

Why don't the cards show pay rates?

Rates depend on the exact live role, your expertise and location, and they change. Our partner platform shows the real terms for each role when you apply — we'd rather send you to the true number than publish one that might not apply to you.

Who pays me, and how?

Our hiring partner does, directly, under the contract terms of the role you accept. JobCorner AI never handles your pay and takes nothing from it.

Is this full-time employment?

No — these are remote contract opportunities. The exact hours, duration and terms are set by each live role, so read the role carefully before accepting.
